# Kanto Team Lab

The builder and Compositions page share Viridian Forest and Pallet Town themes, DM Sans from Google Fonts, game PNGs and item tooltips. Open ../index.html or serve dist/. ../compositions.html is the standalone composition route. The builder supports two to nine units, a nine-slot bench, all seven roles, traits, equipment and evolution skills.

Updated data comes from sim-DVnRY1Xk / roster-CpnLcVHu, captured 21 October 2026. There are six components, 21 finished items, 58 roster lines and 171 reachable line/star BIS states. Flexible abilities use native component counts across their configured stat axes. Crit-enabled abilities are explicitly marked.

BIS scores are frozen picks evaluated on 512 final confirmation battles per build: eight opponent lineups, 32 seeds and both sides. Score counts wins plus half draws. Alternative builds remain accessible with compact score labels. These are conditional battle results, not universal BIS or full-game win rates.

The Compositions route shows provisional S/A/B tiers, strategy and optional BIS-dependent tags, final lineups, battle scores, weakest checkpoints and item sensitivity. Expand a row to inspect its fixed core, level additions, formation, component requirements and unit-copy cost. Each stage can open directly in the builder. Test details explain the scoring limits.

Equipment replacement, branching and removal are planner operations. Actual gameplay can require component acquisition, Eevee merge conditions and Magnets. Economy and full-match placement are not simulated by the website.

Rebuild from the workspace root:

    node outputs/kanto-team-builder/extract-data.mjs
    node outputs/kanto-team-builder/extract-bis.mjs
    node outputs/kanto-team-builder/verify.mjs
    node outputs/kanto-team-builder/verify-bis.mjs
    node outputs/kanto-team-builder/build-standalone.mjs

The composition payload is generated by node outputs/kanto-comps/report.mjs after its search. Static assets are packaged in dist/. Runtime use requires no native engine modules, installation or connection to the live game. The source extraction and verification scripts use the sibling kanto-headless-v2 and kanto-bis-v2 directories.

Verification covers native trait counts, 351 equipment transitions, 2,262 skill/equipment cases, 3,654 cached individual item-fit values and 441 image hashes. The DM Sans font license is in dist/media/fonts/OFL.txt. Art and game assets retain their original ownership.
