        How these results were measured

        A battle score is (wins + half draws) ÷ battles, not a ranked match win rate or placement prediction. Intervals use a paired-seed bootstrap: both sides of a seed stay together. They describe seed variation for these fixed boards, not uncertainty across every possible formation. Near-0% and 100% empirical intervals can collapse when no seed variation was observed; they are not guarantees.

        Replays are median-duration examples within each observed outcome from the published-formation batch. Missing outcomes were not observed. Snapshots are sampled every 0.2 seconds; attacks, abilities, damage, healing, control and eliminations come from the native event stream. Rendering interpolates between snapshots and does not run a second combat engine.

        Stars, items and formations are fixed. No shops, contested pools, component drops, weather or boons are simulated. Tier eligibility follows the existing composition list at each selected level. B-tier builds and self-matchups are excluded. Changing levels can change eligibility.
